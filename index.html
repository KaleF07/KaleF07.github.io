About me

Electrical engineer building practical technology.

Kale is an embedded systems engineer based out of Nanaimo, British Columbia.

He currently works as an Embedded Systems Engineer at VMAC, building safety-critical real time control systems for underhood compressor systems. During his time in this role, he has gained a strong understanding of RTOS applications, firmware debugging and validation, and CAN communication protocol. He is eager to apply his knowledge to new and exciting projects that cover a wide range of applications.

He has a B.Sc in Electrical Engineering from the University of Calgary.
